The Book

Your brain is lying to you right now. It does it constantly, automatically, and usually in ways you never notice.

Fifteen biases across 64 pages, 8.5 by 11 inches. Each one gets a Definition, History, Variant, Usage Note and Example on one page, and a full page to color on the other. Two blank pages after every spread, so nothing bleeds through onto an illustration.

Also a decent gift for the overthinker, the therapist, the student, or the person who is definitely wrong and definitely not listening.

About Bias Brain Press

I color. I train AI.

I got tired of waiting for someone else to make the thing I actually wanted to buy. The coloring book market has roughly 100,000 titles that all share the same DNA: mandalas, flowers, steampunk girls, and swear words, repeat. I searched for something different and found nothing. So I decided to build it myself, but I had one rule, if I'm doing this, it's going to be a quality product. Not a side hustle. Not a quick throw-together. The idea sat for two years while I worked my day job training large language models. I'm into philosophy and human behavior so I thought, a cognitive biases coloring book... Did I mention I can't draw?

What I am good at is prompting AI models with meticulously engineered instructions to make them output exactly what I want. Four years of training AI models taught me how to make machines do precise, repeatable work. So I built an app... well, I instructed an LLM to build it. An app that calls the top image generation APIs. I wrote a set of system instructions that dictates the style of the art before I even say a word. The app scales the image to the correct aspect ratio, and exports print-ready resolution. Two months of iterating prompts and finding the right model for the job. I rebuilt the app from scratch three times, but finally, I was ready to start working.

Only then I realized, a bias happens inside a person's head. How was I going to get this onto a single page, in one frame, with no words? Fifteen times? It never crossed my mind how hard this would be to get right, but I also never intended to just throw together some AI slop. I wanted to make something genuinely interesting and something I'd actually want to color.

This is Bias Brain. Volume 1 is just the start.
